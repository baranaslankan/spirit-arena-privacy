Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

The short version. Spirit Arena has no accounts and needs no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to serve those ads, as described below. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

This policy explains how the Spirit Arena mobile game for iOS and Android (the “App”) handles information. “We”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer of Spirit Arena.

1. No accounts, progress stays on your device

The App has no user accounts, no sign-in and no game servers. The game runs offline. Your progress (coins, unlocked fighters, purchases you have made, settings and any tournament in progress) is saved only in the App’s local storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID on Android and, on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped or watched.

This data goes directly to Google, not to us. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads may be personalised. On iOS the App asks for tracking permission through Apple’s system prompt; if you decline, ads are still shown but without the IDFA.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you buy Remove Ads, the App stops showing ads between matches. Rewarded ads remain available only if you choose to watch one.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads and All Fighters) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play using their own payment systems. We never see or store your name, payment card or billing details. The App only receives confirmation of which products you own, so it can unlock them. You can restore purchases at any time from the App’s shop, which asks the store for your past purchases.

4. No analytics, no selling of data

The App currently contains no analytics or tracking SDK of our own. We do not sell, rent or share personal information. If we add analytics or other services in the future, we will update this policy first.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change how ads use your data at any time:

  • In the App: open the Shop and tap Ad privacy choices to review or change your consent (where Google’s consent form applies).
  • On iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, to allow or deny tracking for Spirit Arena or for all apps.
  • On Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads), to reset or delete your advertising ID.

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698) give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google using the links above; we are glad to help with any question.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App adds new features or services. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
